Pranjal Cake & Bakers is all about spreading sweetness and joy through our delicious range of cakes, pastries, desserts, breads, and baked treats. We believe that every celebration deserves a special touch, and our creations are made to make your moments memorable.

Starting our journey in 2022, Pranjal Cake & Bakers began as a small local bakery with a big dream – to serve fresh, tasty, and beautifully designed cakes to our customers. With love, hard work, and support from our community, we have grown into a trusted name for quality and taste.

Our mission is simple: to bring happiness through every bite. Whether it’s a birthday, anniversary, wedding, or any special occasion, we are here to make it sweeter with our handcrafted baked delights.
